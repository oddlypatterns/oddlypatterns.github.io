The idea behind ODDLY

A space for the curious and the reflective.

Not everything meaningful announces itself. Sometimes, understanding begins with a question we cannot quite put into words.

ODDLY: Of People and Patterns is a personal journal about the connections between people, ideas, experiences, and the worlds we inhabit.

From psychology and literature to science, philosophy, and reflections on Kashmir, this is a space for thoughtful writing without the need to have every answer.

Written and curated by Abrarul Haq.

Stay curious

There is always more beneath the surface.

Read slowly. Question thoughtfully. Notice the patterns. And make room for a different perspective.
