A space for the curious and the reflective.

ODDLY: Of People and Patterns is an independent journal about the connections between people, ideas, experiences and the worlds we inhabit.

From psychology and literature to science, philosophy and reflections on Kashmir, this space welcomes thoughtful writing, honest questions and different perspectives.
